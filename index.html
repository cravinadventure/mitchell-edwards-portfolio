How I work

I built my own
team of AI agents

I built custom AI agents that handle the busywork around the design, and they work together. When I join a team, I learn how you already work first and fit in around it. A few of their skills:

Making the work
1 · Research

Brand breakdowns

Before I design anything, I get a full breakdown of the brand: who they are, who they are up against, and how they look, sound, and post.

2 · Design

Design production

I design one main version. It gets turned into every size I need, like posts, stories, and banners, so they all match without rebuilding each one by hand.

3 · Editing

Video editing

I drop in raw footage. It comes back cut into clips, each one labeled with what happens in it, plus a rough first edit. Months later I can still search for any shot.

4 · Music

Cut to the beat

Clips get cut in time with the song, and a full DJ set comes back as short clips with every track named, ready to post.

Running the work
5 · Planning

Project timelines

Deadlines, deliverables, and next steps stay mapped out in one place, so every project always knows what comes next.

6 · Organization

Files and handoffs

Every file lands in the right folder with a clear name, decisions get written down as they happen, and the right person gets what they need without chasing it.

7 · Publishing

Posting and results

Posts get sized, captioned, and scheduled. Once they are live, the numbers come back with a shot-by-shot read on what made a video work.

8 · Web

Website design

I design the site, and the agents help build it and keep it current, so new work shows up without starting over.

Why I built them
About

Visual Systems Designer

I design visual identities for one specific artist or brand, and build the system that keeps them consistent everywhere they show up.

The part people see: stage visuals, LED and VJ content, 3D logo animation, projection mapping and branding. Every piece is made for that one artist or brand, never pulled from a template.

The part people don't see: clear file names, organized folders and clean handoffs, so anyone on the team can open a project and know exactly what to use.

If there's already a brand or a team in place, I learn how it works first, then design inside it and help it run smoother.

Ten years with musicians, venues and hospitality brands, usually taking projects from first concept to final export on a deadline. I build my own tools, so custom work still moves fast.
