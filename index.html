How I work

I set things up so the busywork runs itself

Every creative team hits the same bottlenecks. Hours of raw footage waiting to be logged. One approved concept that still needs adaptations for every channel and placement. Feedback and approvals scattered across email, Slack, and comments. None of it is design work, but it decides how fast anything ships. So I build tooling that takes those jobs off people.

1 · Editing

Video editing

Footage comes back already logged and organised, with a first assembly waiting in Premiere Pro. The library stays searchable long after the shoot.

2 · Design

Design production

One approved concept becomes every channel adaptation from the same source file, so nothing drifts off spec between versions.

3 · Publishing

Social publishing

Finished work gets sized, captioned and out the door on schedule instead of sitting done and unposted. This cut scheduling time about 75% at a previous job.

Project updates

Decisions and deliveries get written down as they happen, in one place instead of across three inboxes, so the status is already there before anyone asks. Check-ins land on the calendar on their own, and once work goes live the numbers come back in.

About

Multimedia & Brand Designer

I design motion graphics, brand visuals, and social content. I also write the software that speeds up how that work gets made.

Ten years in, mostly with clients in music, hospitality, and events. For most of that I've handled the whole pipeline, from the first concept through the final export and out the door on a deadline.

I'm calm when timelines get short, and I'd rather ship something finished and honest than something clever that missed the point. When a job needs a tool I don't know yet, I go learn it.

When I join a team I start by learning the business before I touch the work. The design gets better once I understand what the thing is actually for.
