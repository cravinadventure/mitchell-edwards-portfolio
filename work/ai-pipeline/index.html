How I work

I built my own
team of AI agents

These are custom AI agents I built. Each one has its own skills, and they work together. They take care of the busywork around the design, so my time goes into the design itself.

01

A few of their skills

Video editing. I drop in raw footage. It comes back cut into clips, each one labeled with what happens in it, plus a rough first edit ready to open. Months later I can still search for any shot by what's in it.

Design production. I design one main version. It gets turned into every size I need, like posts, stories, and banners, without rebuilding each one by hand, so they all match.

Social publishing. Finished posts get sized for each platform, get their captions, and get scheduled to go out on set days, so nothing sits finished and unposted.

Project updates. Every decision and delivery gets written down in one place the moment it happens. Meetings go on the calendar automatically, and after a post goes live, the views and likes come back to me.

That's a sample, not the full list. New skills get added whenever a job keeps repeating.

02

Working with your team

I don't replace what's already there. I start by learning how your people and tools work, then look for the repetitive parts worth taking off their plate. Everything gets built around the software you already use.
