How does it work?

Take a photo of each dish on your phone and send them to us. About a day later you get back finished photos for your delivery apps and your menu.

How much does it cost?

$15 a dish, with a 15 dish minimum. After 40 dishes, each extra dish is $13. Type your number of dishes above to see your price.

Who takes the photos?

You do, on your phone. A cook or server can snap each dish right before it goes out. No skills needed. A real person checks every photo.

How fast is it?

About a day after we get your photos. You can send them all at once or a few at a time.

Do you change the food?

Never. We fix the light and the background. Your food stays exactly how you served it.

Do you use AI?

For some editing steps, yes. Your food never changes, a person checks every photo, and you approve each one.

What do we need to send?

A phone photo of each dish and your menu. That's it.

Our photos aren't very good. Is that OK?

Totally. Any light, any background, no staging.

How do we send them?

Text, email or a shared folder. Whatever's easiest.

Will they work on every delivery app?

Yes. We size every photo for each app. Each app still checks photos before they go live.

What if we don't like a photo?

Tell us and we'll do our best to fix it. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

What about new dishes later?

Send them anytime. We make them match the rest of your photos.

Do you only do photos from above?

Photos from above work best on delivery apps. Want side-angle photos for your website or social too? Just ask.

Do the files belong to us?

Yes. Use them anywhere.

Do you need to be local?

No. It's all remote, so we work with restaurants worldwide.
