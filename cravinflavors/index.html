Common questions

How does it work?

Snap each dish on your phone and text us the photos. We send back clean menu photos, ready for delivery apps, where good photos help dishes sell.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many dishes. Hit Get started and we'll send a quote. No commitment.

Who takes the photos?

You do, on your phone. We recommend a chef or food runner snap each dish as it goes out. No skills needed, no one comes in, nothing to schedule. A real person on our team checks every photo.

How fast is it?

About a day once we have all your photos. Or send dishes as you go.

Do you change the food?

Never. We fix the angle, light and background. Your plating stays exactly as served. Small cleanups can be requested.

Do you use AI?

For some editing steps, yes. Your food and plating never change, a person checks every photo, and you approve each one. Small cleanups can be requested.

What do we need to send?

A phone photo of each dish and your menu. That's it.

Our photos aren't very good. Is that OK?

Totally. Any light, any background, no staging.

How do we send them?

Text, DM or a shared folder. Whatever's easiest.

Will they work on DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub and Toast?

We size every photo for each app. Each app approves photos on its own.

What if we don't like a photo?

Tell us and we'll do our best to fix it. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

What about new dishes later?

Send them anytime and they're finished to match your set.

Do you only do top-down photos?

Top-down is best for delivery apps. Styled shots for social or your website are an add-on.

Do the files belong to us?

Yes. Use them anywhere.

Do you need to be local?

No. It's all remote, so we work with restaurants worldwide.
