Privacy

Last updated September 18, 2026

Cravin' Flavors is a service of Cravin' Adventure Studios. This page explains what we collect when you use this site and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us. When you use the Get started form, that's your restaurant name, your email, and, if you add them, about how many dishes and a short message. The form is delivered to us by email through Google, and we send you one confirmation email. If you message or email us, we receive what you send, including any photos and your menu.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, send a quote and a checklist, and deliver your photos.
  • To keep a record of our work together.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to advertise to you.

Your photos

The photos you send are used to create your finished images. We won't show your photos or name your restaurant in our own marketing without your permission.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives basic technical information like your IP address to deliver them.

Sharing

We only share your information with the tools we need to run the service, like email, and when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us at any time. Email cravinadventure@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
