Common questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many dishes need photos. Hit Get started, tell us about your menu, and we'll send you a quote. No commitment.

How fast is it?

It depends on how fast you send us the photos. Send the whole menu at once and we can turn it around in about a day, or send dishes as you go and we'll keep pace.

What do we need to send?

One phone photo of each dish and a link to your menu. That's it.

Who takes the photos?

You do, with your phone. We never come to your restaurant. We recommend having a chef or food runner snap each dish right before it goes out during a normal day of service. Save them up, then text them all to us at once, or share a folder, whatever's easiest. That's your whole part. From there, a real person on our team works on every photo and sends the finished set back to you.

Our photos aren't very good. Is that OK?

That's expected. Kitchen light and a quick phone shot are fine. If one can't be used, it goes on your checklist to retake.

How do we send them?

Text, DM or a shared folder. Whatever's easiest for your kitchen.

Do you change the food?

No. We adjust the angle, lighting and background. The plating stays exactly how you serve it, and every photo is checked against your original.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as one part of the process. We use a series of editing tools, and some of the enhancement steps use AI. We never change your food or how it's plated. Small cleanups, like a stray crumb or a misplaced garnish, are included. Bigger changes you ask for are quoted separately. Every image starts from a photo of your real dish, a person checks it against your original, and you approve every photo before it goes live.

Will they work on DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub?

We size and format every photo for each app. Each platform reviews photos on its own, so approval is up to them, and we'll help fix any photo that gets flagged.

What if we don't like a photo?

Tell us what's off and we'll do our best to fix it. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

What about new dishes later?

Send them the same way whenever your menu changes, and they're finished to match the rest of your set.

Do you only do top-down photos?

Top-down works best on delivery apps, so we start there. Styled, moody shots for social, your website or a new menu are an add-on, drinks included.

Do the files belong to us?

Yes. Use them on every app, your website, Google, social and print.

Do you need to be local?

No. The whole service runs remotely, so it works for restaurants anywhere.
