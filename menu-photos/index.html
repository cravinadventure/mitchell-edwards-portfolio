Questions

Do you change the food?

No. We adjust the angle, the lighting and the background. The plating stays exactly how you serve it, and every finished photo is checked against your original before it's delivered.

Our photos aren't very good. Is that a problem?

That's expected. Kitchen lighting and a quick phone shot are fine. If a photo can't be used, it goes on your checklist with a note on what to retake.

How fast is it?

Once we have your photos, a full menu takes about a day. Send them all at once for same-day turnaround, or send dishes as they come up and we'll keep pace.

Who takes the photos?

Anyone on staff. It takes a few seconds per plate while the dish is on the pass.

How do we send them?

Text them, DM them or drop them in a shared folder, whatever is easiest for your kitchen.

What about new dishes later?

Send them the same way whenever your menu changes. They're finished to match the rest of your set.

Do you only do top-down photos?

No. Top-down on a clean background is what works best on delivery apps, so that's where we start. We also do styled, on-location shots for social, your website and print, drinks included.

Do the files belong to us?

Yes. Use them on every delivery app, your website, Google, social and print.
