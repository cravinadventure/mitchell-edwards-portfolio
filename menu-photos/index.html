Common questions

Do you change the food?

No. We adjust the angle, lighting and background. The plating stays exactly how you serve it, and every photo is checked against your original.

Our photos aren't very good. Is that OK?

That's expected. Kitchen light and a quick phone shot are fine. If one can't be used, it goes on your checklist to retake.

How fast is it?

About a day for a full menu once we have your photos. Send them all at once, or as you go.

Who takes the photos?

Anyone on staff. A few seconds per plate while it's on the pass.

How do we send them?

Text, DM or a shared folder. Whatever's easiest for your kitchen.

Do you only do top-down photos?

Top-down works best on delivery apps, so we start there. Styled shots for social and your website are an add-on.

Do the files belong to us?

Yes. Use them on every app, your website, Google, social and print.
